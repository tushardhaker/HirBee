HirBee Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: February 04, 2026

This Refund & Cancellation Policy explains how refunds, cancellations, and wallet funds are handled on HirBee. It applies to all users (clients and freelancers) who use HirBee’s payment, wallet, and escrow services.

By using HirBee, you agree to this policy.

1. General Principle

HirBee follows a wallet-based escrow system.

Refunds and cancellations are governed strictly by the status of escrow funding and payment release.

Once payment is released to a freelancer, the transaction is final and non-refundable, except where required by law.
2. Wallet Funds
  • Clients add money to their HirBee Wallet to fund projects or milestones
  • Wallet funds not yet allocated to escrow may be refundable, subject to payment provider rules
  • HirBee may deduct applicable processing or transaction fees
3. Project Cancellation (Before Work Starts)

A project may be cancelled when:

  • Escrow is funded, but
  • The freelancer has not started or submitted any work

In such cases:

  • Escrow funds may be returned to the client’s HirBee Wallet
  • Applicable platform or payment processing fees may be deducted
4. Cancellation During an Ongoing Project

If work has already started:

  • Cancellation eligibility depends on delivery status and dispute resolution
  • Clients must raise a dispute through HirBee before escrow release
  • HirBee may review platform records, submissions, and communication

Partial refunds (if any) are decided case-by-case.

5. Milestone-Based Cancellations
  • Each milestone is treated independently
  • Funded but undelivered milestones may be eligible for refund
  • Completed and approved milestones are non-refundable

Unfunded milestones can be cancelled freely.

6. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be issued only before escrow funds are released, and only when:

  • Work is not delivered
  • A dispute is resolved in the client’s favor
  • Refund is required under applicable law
7. No Refund After Payment Release
No refunds, reversals, or chargebacks are permitted once escrow funds are released to the freelancer, including cases involving:
  • Dissatisfaction after approval
  • Change of mind
  • External agreements
  • Off-platform communication

Clients must review work carefully before approving payment release.

8. Off-Platform Payments & Agreements

HirBee does not provide refunds or protection for:

  • Payments made outside the HirBee platform
  • Direct bank transfers, cash payments, or third-party arrangements
  • Agreements made through WhatsApp, email, phone, or social media

Such actions are taken entirely at the user’s own risk.

9. Fees & Charges
  • Platform, service, or transaction fees are non-refundable
  • Payment gateway fees may be deducted from refunded amounts
  • HirBee is not responsible for fees charged by banks or payment providers
10. Refund Method & Timeline
  • Approved refunds are processed to the HirBee Wallet or original payment method
  • Processing time depends on payment provider and region
  • HirBee does not control external banking delays
11. Abuse & Fraud Prevention

HirBee reserves the right to:

  • Deny refunds in cases of abuse or bad faith
  • Suspend or terminate accounts involved in fraudulent activity
  • Take action to comply with legal or regulatory obligations
12. Policy Changes

HirBee may update this Refund & Cancellation Policy at any time. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
