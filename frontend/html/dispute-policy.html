HirBee Dispute Resolution Policy

Last updated: February 04, 2026

This Dispute Resolution Policy explains how disagreements between clients and freelancers are handled on HirBee. It applies to all disputes related to projects, milestones, payments, and platform usage.

By using HirBee, you agree to follow this dispute process.

1. When a Dispute Can Be Raised

A dispute may be raised only before escrow funds are released, and only when:

  • Work is not delivered
  • Work delivered does not match the agreed scope
  • There is a disagreement about milestones or deadlines

Disputes must be raised within the review period specified on the platform.

2. How to Raise a Dispute
  • Disputes must be submitted through the HirBee platform
  • Disputes raised via email, WhatsApp, or off-platform communication will not be considered
  • Both parties must provide relevant evidence (messages, files, milestones, timelines)
3. Evidence Review

HirBee may review:

  • Platform messages and communication
  • Submitted files and work samples
  • Milestone definitions and project scope
  • Activity logs and timestamps

Only evidence available on the HirBee platform will be considered.

4. HirBee’s Role
  • HirBee acts as a neutral facilitator, not a legal arbitrator
  • HirBee does not guarantee outcomes or user satisfaction
  • Decisions are made based on platform records and policy compliance

HirBee’s decision regarding escrow release or refund is final and binding within the platform.

5. Possible Outcomes

After review, HirBee may:

  • Release escrow funds to the freelancer
  • Refund escrow funds to the client (before release only)
  • Propose a partial release or resolution
  • Reject the dispute due to insufficient evidence or policy violation
6. No Disputes After Payment Release ⚠️

Once escrow funds are released, disputes are permanently closed.

HirBee will not reopen disputes or reverse payments after release.

7. Off-Platform Disputes

HirBee is not responsible for disputes arising from:

  • Off-platform payments
  • External communication or agreements
  • Work shared outside the platform

Such disputes are handled entirely at the users’ own risk.

8. Abuse of Dispute System

HirBee reserves the right to:

  • Reject abusive or fraudulent disputes
  • Suspend or terminate accounts misusing the dispute system
  • Take action required by law or platform safety policies
9. Governing Law

This Dispute Resolution Policy is governed by the laws of India, unless otherwise required by applicable law.
