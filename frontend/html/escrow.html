HirBee Escrow & Payment Protection Policy

Last updated: February 04, 2026

This Escrow & Payment Protection Policy explains how payments, wallet funds, escrow, and refunds work on HirBee. It is designed to protect both clients and freelancers while ensuring fair and transparent transactions.

By using HirBee’s payment features, you agree to this policy.

1. What Is Escrow on HirBee?

HirBee provides an escrow-based payment system to promote trust between clients and freelancers.

The escrow system is designed to:

  • Protect client payments
  • Encourage fair and timely delivery by freelancers
  • Reduce disputes related to payments

Funds placed in escrow are securely held in accordance with this policy until release conditions are met.

2. HirBee Wallet System
  • Clients must first add funds to their HirBee Wallet
  • Wallet funds are securely maintained by HirBee or its authorized payment partners
  • Wallet funds can be allocated to escrow for a specific project or milestone
  • Wallet balances cannot be used outside the HirBee platform

Adding money to the HirBee Wallet does not mean payment to the freelancer until escrow is released.

3. How Escrow Works

Step 1: Project Agreement
The client and freelancer agree on project scope, milestones (if any), and payment amount.

Step 2: Funding Escrow
The client funds the escrow by allocating money from their HirBee Wallet. Escrow must be funded before work begins.

Step 3: Work Delivery
The freelancer completes and submits work through the HirBee platform.

Step 4: Review Period
The client reviews the submitted work within the allowed timeframe.

Step 5: Payment Release
If the client approves the work, escrow funds are released to the freelancer. If the client takes no action within the review period, funds may be automatically released.

4. Milestone Payments
  • Projects may be divided into milestones
  • Each milestone must be funded separately
  • Only funded milestones are eligible for payment release
  • HirBee will not release payment for unfunded milestones
5. Client Responsibilities

Clients agree to:

  • Fund escrow before requesting work
  • Review submissions carefully and promptly
  • Raise disputes within the allowed review period
  • Avoid requesting work outside the agreed scope without additional funding

Failure to respond during the review period may result in automatic escrow release.

6. Freelancer Responsibilities

Freelancers agree to:

  • Deliver work according to the agreed scope and timeline
  • Submit work only through the HirBee platform
  • Address reasonable revision requests that fall within the agreed scope
  • Not request or accept payments outside the HirBee platform
7. Disputes

If a disagreement arises before escrow release:

  • Either party may raise a dispute through HirBee
  • HirBee may review platform records, messages, files, and milestone details
  • HirBee’s role is limited to facilitating resolution and enforcing platform rules

HirBee does not guarantee outcomes and does not act as a legal arbitrator.

8. Fees
  • HirBee may charge service, platform, or transaction fees
  • All applicable fees are clearly displayed before payment confirmation
  • Fees are non-refundable, unless required by applicable law
9. Refunds & Final Payments

Refunds may be issued only before escrow funds are released, and only in the following cases:

  • Work is not delivered
  • A dispute is resolved in the client’s favor
  • Refund is required by law
Once escrow funds are released to the freelancer, the payment is considered complete and final.
After release: No refunds, no reversals, no chargebacks through HirBee.
Clients are responsible for reviewing work carefully before approving payment.
10. Payment Processing & Third Parties
  • Payments are processed by authorized third-party payment providers
  • HirBee does not store full credit card or banking details
  • Processing times may vary depending on provider, method, and region
11. Limitations of Payment Protection

HirBee’s escrow and payment protection does not apply to:

  • Payments or agreements made outside the HirBee platform
  • Off-platform communication or file sharing
  • Scope changes not documented on HirBee
  • Quality expectations not clearly defined
  • Delays caused by client inactivity

Any off-platform payments or agreements are made entirely at the users’ own risk.

12. No Financial Institution Role

HirBee is not a bank or financial institution.

Escrow funds and wallet balances are held and managed through licensed payment partners.

13. Policy Changes

HirBee may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of escrow or wallet services after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
