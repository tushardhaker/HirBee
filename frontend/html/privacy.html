HirBee Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 05, 2026

HirBee (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use our website, applications, and services (collectively, the “Platform”).

By accessing or using HirBee, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information You Provide

We may collect:

  • Name, username, profile photo
  • Email address, phone number
  • Address, timezone, country
  • Identity verification documents (where required)
  • Payment and billing information
  • Messages, projects, bids, reviews, and other content you submit

b. Information Collected Automatically

When you use HirBee, we may collect:

  • IP address and device identifiers
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Pages viewed, actions taken, time spent
  • Approximate location (and GPS only with permission)

c. Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from:

  • Payment processors
  • Identity verification partners
  • Social login providers (Google, LinkedIn, etc.)
2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and operate the HirBee platform
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Facilitate contracts and payments between users
  • Match freelancers with relevant jobs
  • Improve platform security and prevent fraud
  • Provide customer support
  • Send service-related notifications
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process personal data based on:

  • Your consent
  • Performance of a contract
  • Legal obligations
  • Legitimate business interests (platform safety, fraud prevention, improvements)
4. Automated Decisions & Matching

HirBee uses automated systems to:

  • Rank profiles and proposals
  • Recommend jobs or freelancers
  • Detect suspicious or fraudulent activity

These systems are essential to platform functionality.

5. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember preferences
  • Analyze usage
  • Improve performance

You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

6. Sharing of Information

We may share data with:

  • Payment gateways and banking partners
  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Analytics and security providers
  • Legal authorities when required by law

All partners are contractually required to protect your data.

7. International Data Transfers

HirBee operates globally. Your data may be stored or processed outside your country. We ensure appropriate safeguards for international data transfers in compliance with GDPR and Indian law.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to:

  • Provide services
  • Meet legal and accounting requirements
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements
10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion (subject to legal limits)

  • Request data portability

You can manage most data through your account or contact us directly.
